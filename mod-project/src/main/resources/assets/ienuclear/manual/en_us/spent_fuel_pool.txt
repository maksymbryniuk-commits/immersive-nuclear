Spent Fuel Pool (3x3x3)
Cooling Basin for Depleted Fuel Rods

<&spent_fuel_pool_mb>

A 3x3x3 basin built from Reactor Casing & Reactor Glass with 2x Water source blocks in the center shaft and the Spent Fuel Pool controller on the front center.

Operation:
- Exhausted Uranium/Enriched/MOX capsules from Reactors become hot Depleted Fuel Rods.
- Cooling them in the 3x3x3 Pool yields Depleted Uranium Ingots + recovers Empty Fuel Rods (2x faster with Distilled Water!).
- Bury uncooled waste inside a Nuclear Waste Cask underground (out of sunlight).
