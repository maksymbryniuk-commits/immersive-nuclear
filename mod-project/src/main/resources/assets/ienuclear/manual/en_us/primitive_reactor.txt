Primitive Reactor (1x1)
Standalone Starter FE Generator

A single-block nuclear generator requiring no multiblock, water, or steam turbine.
1. Right-click with Heavy Coolant or Water bucket to fill the internal 8,000 mB tank.
2. Insert a fuel capsule and engage the switch (512 - 1536 FE/t).
3. Connect IE HV/MV Wire Connector strictly to the TOP face!
