Heat Exchanger (3x3x5)
Two-Loop Thermodynamics

<&heat_exchanger_mb>

A 3x3x5 shell-and-tube exchanger built with Steel Scaffolding, Steel Sheetmetal, Fluid Pipes, and the Heat Exchanger Controller.

Loops:
- Primary Loop: Hot Coolant (from Reactor Controller) or Liquid Sodium (2x heat transfer!).
- Secondary Loop: Clean Distilled Water boiled into Hot Steam for turbines.
- Warning: Running dry at >= 1000 C ruptures the tubes and contaminates steam!
