=== STEP-BY-STEP FUEL PRODUCTION ===
1. IE Crusher: Raw Uranium -> 2x Yellowcake Uranium (+35% Uranium Dust).

3. IE Refinery: 10 mB UF6 + 5 mB Ethanol -> 10 mB Enriched U-235 Solution.
4. IE Metal Press (mold_rod): 2x Steel Plate -> Empty Fuel Capsule.
5. IE Metal Press (mold_bullet_casing): 1x Uranium Ingot -> 2x Enriched Pellet.
