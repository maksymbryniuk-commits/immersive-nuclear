Cooling Tower (7x10x7)
Closed Steam-Water Cycle

<&cooling_tower_mb>

A monumental 7x10x7 concrete tower built with IE Concrete, Leaded Concrete base, and the Cooling Tower Controller.

Closed Loop:
- Receives Exhaust Steam automatically output by the Steam Turbine (3x3x5).
- Condenses Exhaust Steam 1:1 back into pure Distilled Water and pumps it back into Reactors / Heat Exchangers (+25% Steam output!).
